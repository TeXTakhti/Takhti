LaTeX Editor for Teaching Maths and Physics

Turn a classroom projector into a live, typeset view of your mathematics while you keep the LaTeX source on your own screen.

Write on one screen, show the result on another

  1. Connect a second monitor or classroom projector and configure the operating system to extend your desktop.
  2. Open a LaTeX document and select Takhti Live.
  3. Use the preview pop-out control to open a separate PDF preview window.
  4. Move that preview window to the classroom display.
  5. Keep the editor on your own screen and type into a complete equation environment.

Why this helps in mathematics and physics

Students see formatted equations, while you work with source code, commands and corrections. This can be useful for live derivations, tutorials and walkthroughs of notation-heavy material.

What should instructors prepare?

Install and test the TeX environment before class, keep a working template with required packages, and use a full build to check larger documents before presenting. Preview updates depend on the document and packages in use.
